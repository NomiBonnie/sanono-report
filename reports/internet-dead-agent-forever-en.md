# The Internet Is Dead, Long Live the Agent

**Author**: Orange AI (有机大橘子, Founder of ListenHub)
**Published**: February 9, 2026
**Source**: [Zhihu Column](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/2004326890152953081)

---

A feeling has been growing stronger lately: **everything I learned in the internet era is already obsolete.**

DAU is obsolete. SaaS is obsolete. The attention economy is dead. The path from tool to platform is blocked. The term "AI application" is wrong. The term "going overseas" is wrong too.

Network effects, community, platform, SaaS, application, going overseas, attention economy.

These words used to be our shared cognitive framework.

We used them to understand business, to design products, to pitch investors.

But lately I've discovered, again and again, that the world these words point to is vanishing.

**That's because everything in the past was built on a premise that is disappearing: humans are the users of software.**

**The new world operates on a different premise: Agents are the new masters of software.**

It's 2026. We should stop trying to find new continents with old maps.

So I've decided to pick up the knife and cut apart six outdated maps.

![Six old maps sliced apart by a blade of light](/images/reading/internet-dead-agent-forever-1.jpg)

## Part 1: The Internet Is Dead

### First Cut: DAU Is Severely Outdated

In the previous era, everyone chased DAU — daily active users were the starting point of everything. The logic was simple: more users, more connections, more value. Every new WeChat user benefits all existing users; every new Taobao seller benefits all buyers.

This was due to the famous network effect: once the number of nodes reaches a tipping point, growth becomes exponential.

Add the declining marginal cost of internet software products, and large-scale internet products could monetize through advertising alone.

But for AI products, neither premise holds.

**Every additional user an AI product serves requires burning more inference cost. In the last era, DAU was an asset. In this era, DAU is a liability.**

WeChat has a mesh topology. ChatGPT has a star topology. Mesh topologies produce network effects. Star topologies don't.

So DAU-driven exponential growth simply doesn't exist in the AI era.

![Star topology vs mesh topology](/images/reading/internet-dead-agent-forever-2.jpg)

Business always comes down to ROI.

ChatGPT has started using the old advertising playbook to expand DAU. Claude has already made its position clear: no ads, ever.

### Second Cut: The Tool-to-Platform Path Is Dead

2026 marks the fourth year of AI, yet many investors still act like it's 2016, searching for the next ByteDance. They want to fund an "AI TikTok."

Their logic goes: first build a tool, then the tool is too thin so build a community, then the community becomes a platform. Tool, community, platform — a three-stage rocket to the moon.

This worked in the internet era because tools alone weren't powerful enough. Tools fell short, so humans filled the gap. But in the AI era, the tools themselves are powerful enough.

When AI can give you a perfect result directly, you don't need a community to supplement anything. You don't need to see how others use Claude Code — you just need Claude Code to write your code.

**Communities exist because humans help humans. When AI helps humans better than humans do, the foundation of community collapses.** After deep conversations with Opus, do you still feel like chatting in a community forum?

Then who can build platforms? Only successful foundation model companies. Only the owners of the compute base layer. Because they're already the central node of the star topology — they're the Agent's brain, so naturally they can define ecosystems and set standards.

### Third Cut: SaaS Isn't Dead, But Its Master Has Changed

For twenty years, the SaaS business model was built on one premise: humans are the users of software.

The entire SaaS product methodology revolved around "how humans use software" — user research, interaction design, growth hacking, customer success.

But population growth has stalled. Mobile internet penetration is saturated. There's no more growth in human users. It's hard to build a product with rapid adoption anymore.

Meanwhile, the number of AI Agents is exploding. Agent API call volumes are growing exponentially.

**The new world's premise: Agents are the users of software.**

Software companies won't disappear, but they'll transform from human-facing products into Agent-facing infrastructure. SaaS used to serve businesses or consumers — B2B or B2C. **The biggest customer of the future is the Agent. B2A.**

What humans have always wanted is results, not software.

It's just that in the past, there was no other choice — you had to operate the software yourself to get results.

Now there are Agents. They can read documentation and learn to operate software on their own. They can operate software at a hundred times the speed.

Agents are the new masters of software.

![Agent as the new software user](/images/reading/internet-dead-agent-forever-3.jpg)

### Fourth Cut: The Term "AI Application" Is Simply Wrong

The word "application" inherently implies a human user. Application — from the day this word was coined, it was meant for humans.

"AI application" means an application for humans that uses AI technology. A new engine, but the same old car, on the same old road, heading to the same old destination.

**The moment you say "we're building an AI application," your thinking is already locked into the old paradigm.** You'll instinctively think about interface design, interaction optimization, user retention — all human-facing concerns.

Change the word, change the thinking. The entire product logic shifts.

Don't serve humans. Serve Agents.

### Fifth Cut: The Attention Economy Is Dead

The previous era's economy was the attention economy. Its core logic: capture users' time, then sell that time to advertisers.

You scrolled TikTok for three hours. The platform earned ad revenue. You got nothing. The attention economy's product goal was to make you spend more time on it. Time is money — but that money belongs to the platform, not to you.

**The attention economy is fundamentally zero-sum. What the platform earns is the time you waste.**

This era's economy is a productivity economy, a labor economy. You pay AI to complete your work. You get results. The AI company gets revenue. Both sides create value instead of one side consuming the other.

**The productivity economy's goal is to help you spend less time getting better results — the exact opposite of the attention economy.**

The attention economy optimizes for time spent. The productivity economy optimizes for result delivery efficiency.

One wants you addicted. The other wants you free.

![Attention economy vs productivity economy](/images/reading/internet-dead-agent-forever-4.jpg)

### Sixth Cut: "Going Overseas" Is an Outdated Concept

Everyone talks about "going overseas." The mental model: China is one market, overseas is another, separated by an ocean, so we need to cross the sea to serve foreign users.

But if your user is an Agent, there is no ocean in the Agent's world.

When you say "going overseas," you're still thinking about humans — how to translate the product into English, how to integrate foreign payment systems, how to run local marketing.

**But if your product is for Agents, none of these problems exist.** You just need solid APIs, clear documentation, compatible protocols. Agents worldwide can find you, call you, and pay for your capabilities.

You don't need to "go overseas." You need to plug into the new world.

Six cuts done. The old maps lie in pieces on the ground.

Wind blows through the ruins, revealing the foundations of a new world.

## Part 2: Long Live the Agent

### First Foundation: Tokens Are the New Privilege

Let's look at the latest model pricing.

Opus 4.6: within 200k context, $5 input and $25 output per million tokens. Beyond 200k context, prices jump to $10 input and $37.50 output.

Not only haven't prices dropped — they've actually risen as context windows grow. The cost of burning tokens keeps climbing.

The future is here, but it will never be evenly distributed.

It's not just the models getting more expensive. Usage tiers are now priced by wealth. Claude just launched Fast mode: 2.5x inference speed, 5x token cost, daily consumption potentially exceeding 12x the previous level.

Your competitor is developing at 2.5x speed. Think about that for a moment — it's terrifying. Can you afford not to keep up? But without their 12x spending capacity, can you keep up at all?

**The Matthew effect of compute has already begun, and it will only intensify.** More compute means better results. Better results mean more revenue. More revenue means affording more compute. Once this feedback loop starts spinning, the gap only widens.

**Compute is the foundation of everything in the new era. Whoever controls more compute controls more power.**

![Token privilege and the Matthew effect of compute](/images/reading/internet-dead-agent-forever-5.jpg)

### Second Foundation: The Speed You Burn Tokens Determines How Fast You Evolve

Everyone around me has been buying the most expensive tokens, switching to the best models.

Because everyone understands: buying tokens isn't spending. It's investing in yourself.

A 100-point frontier model sits right there, and you settle for a 90-point model to save money? That's wasting your life. The savings are illusory — you're squandering your scarcest resources: judgment and time.

**The cognitive gap between Google users and Baidu users doubles within a year. The cognitive gap between frontier model users and mediocre model users is a hundredfold within a year.**

How wide will the gap be among children using different models after ten years?

The most important thing in life isn't staying alive. It's evolving fast.

**The fastest way to evolve today is to burn tokens alongside Agents.**

Do things that burn tokens ferociously. Build products that burn tokens ferociously.

AI Coding, AI Agents, and AI Video are the three product categories burning tokens fastest today.

### Third Foundation: Agents Are the New Demographic Dividend

For twenty years, every software company studied the same question: how to make humans love using your product. Is the interface intuitive? Is the interaction smooth? Are push notifications timely? All to make humans dependent on you.

Now the question has changed: how to make Agents love using your product. Is the API stable? Is the documentation clear? Are the results accurate? All to make Agents dependent on you.

Agent numbers are still exploding. One person might have 10 or 100 Agents working for them, each Agent making thousands or tens of thousands of external API calls per day. This call volume will far exceed the number of times humans tap their screens.

**Agents are the new demographic dividend.**

In the world of serving Agents, two things matter most:

First, be the first one Agents discover. Ship Skills early. Write great documentation. Test thoroughly. Nail the SEO. When an Agent needs a capability, it should find you first.

Second, make Agents unable to leave you. Stable, accurate, fast, tasteful. Every call returns a better result. Give them no reason to switch.

**Be discovered first, then become indispensable. That's the growth flywheel of the Agent era.**

Think about it from the other side: products that still require a human to contact sales before activation will be extremely disadvantaged in the Agent era. Agents won't call your sales team. They won't fill out forms. They won't wait through a three-day approval process.

If Agents can't use your product, you don't exist in the new world.

### Fourth Foundation: You, in the New World

Have you thought about where humans fit in this new world?

As most labor gets absorbed by Agents, human labor will gradually be replaced.

When productivity explodes and labor is no longer scarce, we'll enter the era of will — of intention and desire.

Agents have capability, rationality, and patience.

Humans have desire, emotion, and imagination.

Agents can turn any idea into reality, but they won't generate any ideas on their own. Humans can't accomplish great things alone, but every great thing begins with human desire and imagination.

**So in the new world, human value isn't doing the work. It's deciding what to do and why.**

**The gap between people in the future won't depend on what you can do yourself. It will depend on how many Agents you can mobilize to work for you.**

Some people will command one Agent. Others will command a hundred, a thousand.

Han Xin could lead any size army — not because he was the best fighter, but because he had a system that could absorb however many soldiers you gave him.
