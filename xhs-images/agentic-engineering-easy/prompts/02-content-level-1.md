Create a Xiaohongshu (Little Red Book) style infographic following these guidelines:

## Image Specifications
- **Type**: Infographic
- **Orientation**: Square (1:1)
- **Style**: Hand-drawn chalk illustration on blackboard

## Core Principles
- Hand-drawn chalk quality throughout - NO realistic or photographic elements
- Chalk dust effects, imperfect lines, eraser smudges
- Clear visual hierarchy with colored chalk

## Text Style (CRITICAL)
- ALL text MUST be hand-drawn chalk style, ALL IN CHINESE (中文)
- Titles in bright yellow chalk, key points in white/colored chalk
- DO NOT use computer-generated fonts

## Style: Chalkboard
- Background: Dark chalkboard green-black
- Text: Chalk white, yellow, pink, blue, green accents
- Sketchy imperfect lines, chalk dust, doodles

## Layout: Balanced
3-4 key points, 40-50% whitespace

## Content
**Position**: Content (Page 2 of 10)
**Title** (黄色粉笔大字): 「Level 1 ✦ 打字快了一点」

**Key Points** (白色粉笔):
- AI帮你自动补全句子
- 你打"亲爱的张" → 它补上"总，您好"

**Bottom tag** (粉色粉笔): 🏷️ 像一个安静的速记员
