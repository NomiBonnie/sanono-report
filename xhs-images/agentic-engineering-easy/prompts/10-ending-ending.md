Create a Xiaohongshu (Little Red Book) style infographic following these guidelines:

## Image Specifications
- **Type**: Infographic, Square (1:1)
- **Style**: Hand-drawn chalk illustration on blackboard

## Core Principles
- Hand-drawn chalk quality, NO realistic elements. Chalk dust effects, imperfect lines.

## Text Style (CRITICAL)
- ALL text hand-drawn chalk style, ALL IN CHINESE (中文). Titles in bright yellow chalk. DO NOT use computer fonts.

## Style: Chalkboard (dark green-black board, colored chalk accents)
## Layout: Sparse (Ending — maximum impact)

## Content
**Position**: Ending (Page 10 of 10)
**Title** (黄色粉笔大字): 「你在哪一级？」

**Key Quote** (白色粉笔):
你的效率 ≠ 你的段位
而是团队最慢那个人的段位

**CTA** (粉色粉笔):
💡 真正的杠杆：拉着身边的人一起升级

**Interaction** (蓝色粉笔):
评论区说说你在第几级？👇

**Visual Concept**:
The full 8-level staircase drawn across the board, each step labeled with its level name in small text:
1.打字 2.聊天 3.资料 4.记忆 5.工具 6.自检 7.睡觉干活 8.团队协作

A large question mark ❓ drawn in glowing yellow chalk hovering over the middle of the staircase. At the bottom, the key quote in larger white chalk. The CTA in pink chalk with a lightbulb icon. Stars and sparkles decorating the corners. Clean, impactful, memorable ending.
