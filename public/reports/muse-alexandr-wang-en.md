# Cleo Abram × Alexandr Wang: When AI Starts Acting for You

**Guest**: Alexandr Wang, Meta AI Chief · **Host**: Cleo Abram · **Duration**: 68 minutes · **Video**: [YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1LRvMRC_pfY) · **Date**: October 7, 2026

> Cleo Abram uses Muse, a consumer agent discussed here with Meta AI Chief Alexandr Wang, to ask a larger question: what changes when software moves from answering questions to acting in the world on a person’s behalf? The interview follows that question through data access, discretion, alignment, regulation and personal ambition.
>
> For product leaders, the most practical point is that an agent’s real interface is not a chat box. It is the moment a person grants it access to an inbox, lets it make a call, or trusts it to know when not to act. Capability only becomes useful when its boundaries are intelligible.
>
> Wang’s vision is “personal superintelligence,” an assistant that helps more people pursue more of what they want from life. This interview does not prove that vision. It does make the condition for it unusually clear: agents need to be useful without becoming inscrutable, persuasive without becoming manipulative, and autonomous without crossing a user’s line.
