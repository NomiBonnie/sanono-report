### 1. From Memex to LLM Wiki: 80 Years of Knowledge Management Dreams
In 1945, Vannevar Bush's seminal article "As We May Think" introduced the concept of the "Memex," a revolutionary device resembling a personal, externalized brain. The Memex aimed to replicate mental association trails, promising a future where knowledge documentation becomes a vibrant web of textual and visual interconnections. This vision laid the groundwork for hypertext, the internet, and modern databasing—core to today's AI-driven systems.
